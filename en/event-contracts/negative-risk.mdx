Negative Risk is a mechanism for multi-outcome events where only one outcome can ultimately win. It connects multiple Markets under the same Event through conversion relationships between outcomes, improving capital efficiency.

## How It Works

In a standard multi-outcome event, each Market is independent. If you want to express that a specific outcome will not happen, you would normally buy that Market's No Token, but that No Token has no asset-conversion relationship with the other outcomes.

Negative Risk creates that relationship:

- 1 No share from any Market can be converted into 1 Yes share in every other Market under the same Event.
- The conversion is performed through the Neg Risk Adapter contract.

### Example

Assume the Event is "Who will win the presidential election?" and it contains three outcomes:

| Outcome | Current Position |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate A | — |
| Candidate B | — |
| Other | 1 No |

Under Negative Risk, 1 No share of "Other" can be converted into:

| Outcome | After Conversion |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate A | 1 Yes |
| Candidate B | 1 Yes |
| Other | — |

Economically, being short one outcome can therefore be converted into being long all other outcomes.

## Augmented Negative Risk

Standard Negative Risk generally requires the complete set of outcomes to be known when the market is created. In real-world scenarios, however, new candidate outcomes may appear after trading begins, such as a new candidate entering an election.

Augmented Negative Risk handles these dynamic changes with the following outcome types:

| Outcome Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Named outcomes | Outcomes explicitly defined when the market is created |
| Placeholder outcomes | Reserved slots that can later be assigned to specific outcomes |
| Other | Captures outcomes that are not explicitly listed |

### How Placeholders Work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Event Launches">
    The Event launches with named outcomes, Placeholder outcomes, and Other.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign a Placeholder">
    When a new valid outcome appears, a Placeholder can be assigned to that outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Other Narrows">
    As Placeholders are assigned, the remaining set represented by Other becomes narrower.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Trading Rules

- If the final correct outcome was never explicitly named, the Market can settle to Other.
- The practical definition of Other changes as Placeholders are assigned, so traders should pay particular attention to its current scope when trading Other directly.

## Conversion Mechanism

The Neg Risk Adapter performs conversion atomically:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hold a No Token">
    The user holds 1 No Token for Outcome A.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Call convert">
    Call the Adapter's `convert` method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Yes Tokens for Other Outcomes">
    The user receives 1 Yes Token for every other outcome under the same Event.
  </Step>
</Steps>
