## Before trading

Confirm the market rules, outcome token, current price, order-book depth, fees, and settlement conditions.

## Place an order

1. Open a market.
2. Select an outcome.
3. Choose Buy or Sell.
4. Enter price and size, or use the available market-order flow.
5. Review the order.
6. Submit and wait for the order status to update.

After execution, review the position and trade history.
