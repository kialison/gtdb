description: "Trading flows available in Event Contracts."

The trading flow combines market discovery, order-book data, order entry, order management, trades, positions, and settlement.

## Main flow

```text
Discover market
   ↓
Review outcomes and order book
   ↓
Place order
   ↓
Track order and trades
   ↓
Track position
   ↓
Resolution and settlement
```
