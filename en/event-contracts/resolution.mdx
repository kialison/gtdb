Once an event outcome is known, the Market enters the resolution process. Resolution determines the final winning outcome so that the corresponding outcome token can be redeemed for `1 USDT` per share, while losing outcome tokens become worthless.

Event Contracts can use the UMA Optimistic Oracle for decentralized outcome confirmation. Any eligible participant can propose an outcome and can dispute a result they believe is incorrect.

![Image](/images/image-5.png "Image")

## Resolution Rules

Before trading begins, each Market should define its resolution rules, including:

- **Resolution Source**: the data source used to determine the outcome, such as an official announcement or specified website;
- **End Date**: when the time condition for resolution is satisfied;
- **Edge Cases**: how exceptional, ambiguous, or special situations are handled.

<Note>
Read the complete resolution rules before trading. The market title is only a short summary of the question; the specific Resolution Rules determine how the market is actually settled.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit a Proposal">
    The proposer selects the final outcome, posts a bond, and submits the Proposal to the UMA Oracle.

    If the result is correct and no one disputes it, the proposer can recover the bond and receive the applicable reward.

    <Warning>
    Submitting an incorrect outcome or proposing too early can result in loss of the bond. Only submit a Proposal after confirming the result and understanding the resolution rules.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge Period">
    After the Proposal is submitted, a challenge period begins and other participants can dispute the result.

    - **No challenge**: the Proposal is accepted and the Market resolves.
    - **Challenge occurs**: another Proposal round begins; if disputes continue, the process can escalate to UMA DVM voting.

    Common flows include:

    1. No dispute: Proposal → Resolve.
    2. One dispute: Proposal → Challenge → Second Proposal → Resolve.
    3. Multiple disputes: further escalation to UMA voting for the final result.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dispute">
    Raising a dispute generally requires posting a Counter Bond corresponding to the proposer's bond. If the dispute escalates, the process can enter an evidence and discussion stage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="UMA Voting">
    When a dispute reaches the DVM, UMA Token holders vote on the final result.

    | Result | Handling |
    | --- | --- |
    | Proposal is correct | Original Proposal is accepted |
    | Disputer is correct | Original Proposal is rejected and the result must be determined again |
    | Too Early | The event has not yet met the resolution conditions |
    | Unknown / 50-50 | Rare cases that cannot be determined clearly may use special settlement rules |
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After Resolution

Once the Market is resolved:

- trading stops;
- each winning outcome token can be redeemed for `1 USDT`;
- each losing outcome token is worth `0`.

### Redeeming Outcome Tokens

Users can burn winning ERC-1155 outcome tokens through the relevant CTF / Collateral Adapter and receive the released USDT collateral.

## Resolution Timing

Actual resolution time depends on whether disputes occur:

| Stage | Typical Duration |
| --- | --- |
| Challenge Period | About 2 hours |
| Debate Period (if disputed) | About 24–48 hours |
| UMA Voting (if escalated) | About 48 hours |

Resolution is generally faster when no dispute occurs. With multiple dispute rounds, the full process may take several days.
