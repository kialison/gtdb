## Product model

A event contract represents a defined event or outcome set. Each market exposes clear rules, available outcomes, trading state, important times, and the conditions used for final settlement.

Users trade outcome tokens through the order book. Prices reflect the market's current view of each outcome.

## Lifecycle

A typical lifecycle is:

1. Market published
2. Trading opens
3. Orders and trades update the order book
4. Trading closes when required
5. The event is resolved
6. Eligible positions are settled or claimed
