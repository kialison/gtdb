DexBuilder provides a complete DEX product. Most projects can create, configure, and launch a DEX directly through Builder without separately building a trading frontend or trading infrastructure.

## Build a DEX

DexBuilder supports:

- DEX name, logo, theme, and domain configuration;
- PerpDEX, Prediction Markets, and Event Contracts;
- accounts, assets, deposits, withdrawals, and trading configuration;
- markets and trading parameters;
- fees and revenue settings;
- management-console permissions;
- DEX creation, updates, and deployment.

## API / WebSocket

Use REST API and WebSocket when DexBuilder data or trading capabilities need to be connected to other systems.

Typical use cases include:

- market and price data;
- account, asset, order, and position queries;
- trading operations;
- real-time order, trade, position, and balance updates;
- integration with internal systems.
