---
title: "Fees & Revenue"
description: "Builder-level trading fee, revenue, and settlement settings."
---

Fees and revenue are **project-level Builder configuration** and are separate from ordinary user fee queries.

## Confirm before launch

- user trading fees;
- DexBuilder base fees;
- Builder revenue share;
- special or campaign fee rules;
- revenue-calculation scope;
- settlement cycle and settlement method.

## Fee relationship

```text
User trading
   ↓
Trading fee paid by user
   ↓
Platform base fee + Builder revenue
```

Actual rates, revenue share, and settlement rules follow the final project configuration.

## Builder capabilities

Builder-level capabilities can include:

- current fee configuration;
- user or user-group fee settings;
- fee revenue;
- volume and fee statistics;
- revenue-settlement records.

<Warning>
Only capabilities formally available in the Builder console or API Reference should be treated as supported external features.
</Warning>
