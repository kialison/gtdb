title: "Operations & Technical Support"
description: "Operational monitoring, troubleshooting, and technical support after launch."
---

After launch, DexBuilder continues to provide the DEX product, trading services, API, WebSocket, account, asset, and platform support required for operation.

## Monitoring

Monitor:

- DEX and core-feature availability;
- API and WebSocket status;
- market, order, position, and asset state;
- deposit and withdrawal status;
- failed trades and exceptions;
- project-level operational data;
- administrator and permission changes.

## Troubleshooting

Useful information includes:

1. user or account identifier;
2. event time;
3. market or asset;
4. Order ID / Transaction Hash;
5. response and error code;
6. Trace ID or relevant logs;
7. current business state.

## Platform support

DexBuilder provides support for:

- DEX product and trading services;
- API and WebSocket troubleshooting;
- account, asset, trading, and settlement issues;
- platform incidents and service-status communication;
- project configuration and launch support.
