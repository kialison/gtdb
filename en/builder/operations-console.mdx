The Builder Management Console is designed for project operations and administrators rather than end users.

## Capabilities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Operational data" icon="chart-column">
    Trading volume, trade count, trading addresses, fees, active users, deposits, withdrawals, and traffic metrics.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Users & growth" icon="users">
    User, volume, invitation, and referral data for daily operations and growth analysis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Announcements & content" icon="bullhorn">
    Manage project announcements and announcement categories.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Funds & transfers" icon="wallet">
    Review transfers, deposits, withdrawals, and related states.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Permissions" icon="user-shield">
    Manage administrators, roles, and access scopes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Audit log" icon="clipboard-list">
    Review important administrative actions for troubleshooting and auditing.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Dashboard metrics

Typical project-level metrics include:

- total trading volume and trade count;
- trading addresses and active users;
- fee revenue;
- deposit and withdrawal amounts;
- new users;
- referral data;
- PV / UV and unique IPs;
- volume, user, and fee trends.
