DexBuilder provides the complete asset and transfer experience used by a DEX.

## Asset capabilities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Asset overview" icon="wallet">
    Display total assets, available assets, locked assets, and related balances.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deposits" icon="arrow-down">
    Provide network, address, asset selection, and deposit-status information.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Withdrawals" icon="arrow-up">
    Provide network, destination, amount, fee, and withdrawal-status information.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Account book" icon="list">
    Query deposit, withdrawal, trading, and other balance-change records.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Configuration

A DEX can configure supported assets and networks, minimum amounts, withdrawal fees, and production-environment settings.
