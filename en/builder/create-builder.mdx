---
title: "Create a Builder"
description: "Create a Builder identity and initialize your DEX project."
---

A Builder is the project-level identity used by DexBuilder to associate DEX configuration, user attribution, trading data, fees, the management console, and future Builder/Broker capabilities.

## Before you create a Builder

Prepare:

- project and brand information;
- products to enable;
- target markets and service regions;
- DEX domain and environment;
- project contacts;
- sandbox and production requirements.

## What the Builder identity is used for

- DEX configuration
- User attribution
- Order and trade attribution
- Trading-volume and fee data
- Builder management console
- Project-level operational capabilities

## Creation flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter project information">
    Provide the project name, brand, and basic information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select products">
    Enable PerpDEX, Prediction Markets, Event Contracts, or a combination.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the Builder">
    DexBuilder creates the Builder identity and project configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue configuring the DEX">
    Configure branding, markets, accounts, fees, and operational settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>
