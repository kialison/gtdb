DexBuilder provides the full trading experience from market data and order entry through trades, positions, margin, and account updates.

## Trading flow

```text
Market data
   ↓
Select market
   ↓
Enter and submit order
   ↓
Order / trade updates
   ↓
Position and margin updates
```

## Core capabilities

### Markets and trading rules

Market lists, price precision, quantity precision, order rules, and product parameters.

### Order entry and management

Place, cancel, and amend orders; query current and historical orders; display order status.

### Trades

Market trades and user trade history, with continuous execution updates.

### Positions and margin

Positions, unrealized PnL, margin, leverage, position modes, and related status.
