Prediction Markets trading connects signed CLOB orders with on-chain settlement. Users select an Outcome Token, authorize and sign the order, and submit it to the order book. After matching, settlement transfers USDT and outcome tokens between accounts.

## Trading Flow

Account initialization usually only needs to be completed once. For subsequent orders, users repeat the process of selecting an outcome, placing an order, and managing that order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the Account">
    Connect the signing wallet to the account, prepare trading funds, and complete the required contract allowances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select an Outcome">
    Choose the Outcome Token to buy or sell and confirm the Market's current trading restrictions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place the Order">
    Set the price and quantity, sign the order, and submit it to the CLOB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage the Order">
    Monitor fills and cancel any remaining unfilled quantity. After matching, the trade settles on-chain, and account balances and positions are then updated.
  </Step>
</Steps>
