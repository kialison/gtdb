DexBuilder provides a unified foundation for multiple decentralized market products. Different product types share the same account, asset, data, and Builder capabilities.

## PerpDEX

PerpDEX supports continuous order-book trading without a fixed expiry and includes the main workflows required by active and professional traders:

- contract information, order books, trades, candlesticks, market statistics, and funding history;
- limit, market, reduce-only, post-only, IOC, FOK, batch, and BBO order flows;
- one-way and hedge position modes;
- cross and isolated margin modes;
- leverage, position margin, mark price, funding, liquidation, and ADL records;
- account status, account book, trading fees, personal trades, and close history.

## Prediction Markets

Prediction Markets organize trading around clearly defined questions and possible outcomes. Market pages present the rules, trading state, outcome tokens, key times, and final resolution result so users can understand what they are trading before placing an order.

## Event Contracts

Event Contracts are outcome-based contracts linked to a specific event. Each contract presents explicit terms, lifecycle states, trading availability, and settlement conditions.

## Shared product principles

| Principle | Experience |
| --- | --- |
| Clear market definition | Terms, market state, supported actions, and settlement conditions are visible before trading |
| Consistent account state | Orders, positions or exposure, balances, fees, and history remain coherent across the product |
| Explicit authorization | Authenticated actions and asset operations follow documented credential and wallet authorization rules |
| Traceable lifecycle | Applications can follow a request from submission through execution, account updates, and settlement |
| Configurable Builder experience | Product configuration, market presentation, and Builder-level settings can be adjusted per project |
