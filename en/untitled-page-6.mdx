---
title: "Spot Trading"
---

DexBuilder Spot Trading provides decentralized spot trading through an on-chain order-book model. Users connect a wallet and trade supported assets directly within the DEX.

Perpetual and spot products can be offered in the same DEX. The platform currently supports a broad range of crypto and stock spot assets, with additional markets planned over time.

## Spot account deposits and withdrawals

- USDT is currently the supported deposit and withdrawal asset.
- Before the first spot trade, transfer USDT from the perpetual account to the spot account.
- Other tokens can be held in the spot account but are not currently withdrawable directly.
- To withdraw another token, convert it to USDT, transfer the USDT to the perpetual account, and then withdraw.
