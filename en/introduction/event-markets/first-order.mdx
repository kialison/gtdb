title: "Place Your First Order on an Event Market"
sidebarTitle: "Place Your First Order"
description: "Learn how to authenticate through the gateway and submit your first order on an Event Market."
---

This guide shows you how to place your first order using an existing Event Market account. Authentication details are gateway-managed; do not send internal identity headers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authenticate through the gateway">
    Complete the gateway authentication flow for the account that owns the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an Outcome">
    Then, fetch the market and select the outcome you want to buy. Orders identify each outcome by its token ID. See [Market Data](/en/introduction/event-markets/prices-and-orderbooks) to find a different market.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place an Order">
    Submit a small order through `POST /api/v4/event_markets/orders` and keep the returned order ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the order">
    Read the order and user-trade endpoints to determine whether the order is open, filled, cancelled, or expired.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Your Position">
    Finally, list your positions for the selected market and find the outcome you bought.
  </Step>
</Steps>
