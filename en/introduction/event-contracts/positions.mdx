Position and account endpoints are private and require gateway authentication.

- GET https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/positions
- GET https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/positions/settled
- GET https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/balance
- GET https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/fund_flows

The positions list supports event_id, condition_id, settlement_status, limit, and cursor.
