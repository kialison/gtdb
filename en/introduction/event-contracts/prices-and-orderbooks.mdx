---
title: "Prices and Order Books"
sidebarTitle: "Prices and Order Books"
description: "Read Event Contract token prices, tickers, order books, and price history."
---

```bash
curl "https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/tokens/<token_id>/price"
curl "https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/tokens/<token_id>/ticker"
curl "https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/tokens/<token_id>/order_book?depth=20"
curl "https://broker.dexbuilder.com/api/v4/event_contract/tokens/<token_id>/price_history?interval=5m&from=<from_ms>&to=<to_ms>"
```

`depth` defaults to 20 and accepts 1–100. Price history accepts `5m`, `15m`, `1h`, `4h`, and `1d`; the time range may not exceed 14 days.
