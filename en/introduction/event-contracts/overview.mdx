Event Contracts are available under the /api/v4/event_contract namespace.

Market-data endpoints are public. Orders, user trades, positions, settled positions, balance, fund flows, and fees are private and require gateway authentication.

Start with [Event Contract API](/en/introduction/event-contracts/api) and [Discover Markets](/en/introduction/event-contracts/discover-markets).
