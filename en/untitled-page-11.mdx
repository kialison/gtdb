---
title: "Margin"
---

Margin is the capital required to open and maintain a perpetual position.

## Position margin

```text
Position Margin = Initial Position Margin + Manual Margin Adjustments
```

In isolated mode, margin can be added or removed as long as the remaining margin does not fall below the required initial margin.

## Initial margin

```text
Initial Margin = Position Value / Leverage + Closing Fee
```

## Maintenance margin

```text
Maintenance Margin = Position Value × Maintenance Margin Rate + Closing Fee
```

The maintenance margin rate depends on the position tier. Larger positions may use higher maintenance margin rates and lower maximum leverage.
