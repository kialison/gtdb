DexBuilder is infrastructure for decentralized trading products. It brings market connectivity, order execution, accounts, asset flows, settlement, and Builder operations into one unified system, so teams do not need to build every layer independently before launching a branded trading product.

The same platform can support a complete branded trading experience as well as API-focused integrations, allowing each project to choose the operating model that best fits its product.

## One platform, multiple market experiences

DexBuilder supports three product types: **PerpDEX**, **Prediction Markets**, and **Event Contracts**.

## Built for professional trading products

DexBuilder combines familiar order-book trading with the account, asset, and operational capabilities required by production-grade trading applications.

- **Trading infrastructure:** market data, order management, positions, margin modes, funding, liquidation, and trade history.
- **Consistent account records:** balances, orders, trades, positions, and account-book changes are exposed through a unified account model.
- **Flexible product configuration:** brands, market presentation, supported products, and integration options can be configured per DEX.
- **Builder operations:** user registration, fee configuration, campaigns, referrals, project data, and revenue settlement.
- **Developer-first integration:** bilingual guides, OpenAPI, request signing, code examples, WebSocket guides, and production checklists.
