---
title: "Order Book"
---

The order book shows active buy and sell orders in real time and is the primary view for understanding market depth and nearby liquidity.

- **Price levels:** asks and bids are displayed by price level, usually with the main levels closest to the market.
- **Order value:** shows the total order value at a specific price level.
- **Cumulative amount / size:** shows the accumulated value or size through deeper levels.
- **Spread:** shows the difference between the best ask and best bid, including the percentage spread.
- **Depth visualization:** background bars visualize the relative depth available at each level.

## Interaction

- **Click a price:** fills the selected price into the order-entry panel.
- **Hover a level:** shows more detail, including average price, total asset amount, and USDT value.
- **Real-time updates:** the order book refreshes continuously to reflect the latest market state.
