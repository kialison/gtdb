---
title: "Order Types"
---

DexBuilder supports multiple order types so users can choose the execution behavior that matches their strategy.

## Market order

Executes immediately against the best available prices.

- **Advantage:** fast execution.
- **Trade-off:** execution price is not guaranteed and may be affected by slippage.

## Limit order

Executes only at the specified price or a better price.

- **Advantage:** precise price control.
- **Trade-off:** execution is not guaranteed.

## Advanced limit order

Additional execution instructions can be applied to limit orders:

- **GTC:** remains active until fully filled or cancelled.
- **IOC:** executes immediately and cancels any unfilled quantity.
- **FOK:** must fill completely at once or be cancelled.
- **Post Only:** can only be added to the order book as a maker order; otherwise it is cancelled.

## Order management

Open orders and positions can be reviewed from the trading interface.

- Filter by market, order type, and status.
- Amend or cancel eligible open orders.
- Close all or part of an existing position.
