---
title: "Advanced Order Strategies"
---

Advanced order tools support more complex trading workflows.

## Conditional orders

A conditional order is triggered when the market reaches a predefined condition.

- Stop-limit orders
- Trailing stop orders
- Trigger price plus order price

## Strategy orders

- **Iceberg:** splits a large order into smaller visible portions.
- **Segmented execution:** builds or reduces a position across predefined price intervals.
- **Time-sliced execution:** executes a strategy across a defined time window.

## Reduce Only

A reduce-only order can only reduce or close an existing position and cannot increase exposure.

## Take Profit and Stop Loss

Take-profit and stop-loss orders automatically close positions when the configured trigger condition is reached.

- Trigger by price, return percentage, price movement, or PnL amount.
- Multiple take-profit or stop-loss targets can be applied to different portions of a position.
