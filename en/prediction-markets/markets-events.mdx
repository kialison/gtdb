---
title: "Markets & Events"
description: "How events and markets are organized in Prediction Markets."
---

Events group one or more related markets. A market defines a tradable question or contract with one or more outcome tokens.

Applications should display:

- event title and description;
- market rules and resolution source;
- market status;
- outcome tokens;
- opening and closing times;
- settlement or resolution state.

Use the REST API to discover events and markets, then use WebSocket for real-time updates where available.
