---
title: "Prices & Order Book"
description: "How prices and order-book liquidity work in Prediction Markets."
---

Outcome prices are formed from active buy and sell orders.

The order book shows available liquidity by price level. Applications can use the best bid, best ask, spread, and depth to present current market conditions.

Historical price data and candlesticks can be used for charts, while live order-book updates should be consumed through the available real-time channels.
