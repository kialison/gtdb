## What is the funding rate?

Funding is a periodic payment exchanged between long and short position holders. Its purpose is to help perpetual contract prices remain aligned with the underlying reference market.

- Positive funding: longs pay shorts.
- Negative funding: shorts pay longs.
- Funding payments are exchanged between traders rather than collected as a trading fee by the platform.

Funding intervals can vary by contract, such as 8-hour or 4-hour cycles.

## Funding-rate calculation

A simplified representation is:

```text
Premium Index =
[Max(0, Impact Bid - Index Price) - Max(0, Index Price - Impact Ask)] / Index Price
```

```text
Funding Rate =
Average Premium Index + clamp(Interest Interval - Current Premium Index, 0.05%, -0.05%)
```

The final funding rate is bounded by the configured maximum funding range for the market.

## Impact prices

Impact bid and ask prices are calculated from the order book using a configured notional depth. They represent the average executable price needed to trade the target notional amount.
