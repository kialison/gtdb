---
title: "Mark Price"
---

Mark Price is designed to provide a stable reference price for unrealized PnL and liquidation calculations.

## Calculation

The final mark price uses the median of multiple reference prices:

```text
Mark Price = median(Price 1, Price 2, Last Traded Price)
```

Where:

- **Price 1** is derived from the index price and funding basis.
- **Price 2** is derived from the spot index and a moving-average basis.

Additional safeguards can limit short-lived abnormal deviations before normal mark-price updates resume.

## Uses

Mark Price is used for:

- unrealized PnL;
- position value;
- liquidation calculations;
- margin-related calculations.
