---

**DexBuilder** provides complete DEX creation and management capabilities. After connecting a wallet, you can select **PerpDEX, Prediction Markets, Event Contracts**, or a combination of products, then configure branding, market settings, and service options before launch.

## Create a DEX

<Steps>
  <Step title="Configure basic DEX information">
    Enter the DEX name and select the products to enable:
    - PerpDEX
    - Prediction Markets
    - Event Contracts
    - Multi-product combinations
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure branding and interface">
    Set the logo, brand name, theme, social links, and the page presentation provided by DexBuilder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure markets and trading">
    Configure supported networks, markets, trading instruments, and relevant market parameters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure language and service settings">
    Set supported languages, service regions, limits, and required service notices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and deploy the DEX">
    Once the required configuration is complete, create the DEX. DexBuilder generates and deploys the corresponding trading interface and services.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Supported products

| Product | Description |
| --- | --- |
| PerpDEX | Perpetual trading with market data, orders, positions, and accounts |
| Prediction Markets | Outcome-based markets around defined events |
| Event Contracts | Event-based contract markets with clear settlement outcomes |
| Multi-product DEX | Enable multiple DexBuilder products in one DEX |

## DEX management

After creation, **My DEX** can be used to update:

- branding and theme;
- fee settings and Builder revenue configuration;
- enabled products and markets;
- deployment and update tasks;
- deployment status.
