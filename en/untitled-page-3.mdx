title: "Core Capabilities"
description: "Trading, assets, Builder operations, and developer capabilities available on DexBuilder."
---

DexBuilder reduces the number of independent systems a project needs to build and maintain. Its capabilities are organized into four connected areas.

## Trading experience

- Order-book market data and real-time streams
- Professional order types and batch order management
- One-way and hedge position modes
- Cross and isolated margin modes
- Mark price, funding, liquidation, insurance fund, and ADL information
- Account, trade, close, and fee history

## Accounts, assets, and settlement

- Wallet-based authorization
- Account registration and account-status queries
- Deposits, withdrawals, and internal transfers
- Account-book and reconciliation flows
- PnL and Builder revenue settlement

## Builder operations

- Project registration and Builder information
- User registration and authorized user data
- Fee configuration and order attribution
- Campaigns and referral programs
- Project-scoped administrative data access

## Developer platform

- Public and authenticated REST operations
- WebSocket integration
- HMAC-SHA512 request signing and credential permissions
- Idempotent request patterns
- Chinese and English documentation
- OpenAPI definitions for client generation and validation
- Troubleshooting and production-launch guidance
