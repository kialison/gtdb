---
title: "Event Contracts Account Data"
sidebarTitle: "Account Data"
description: "Read authenticated Event Contracts orders, trades, positions, balances, and fund flows."
---

These endpoints are private and require gateway authentication.

```http
GET /api/v4/event_contract/usertrades
GET /api/v4/event_contract/positions
GET /api/v4/event_contract/positions/settled
GET /api/v4/event_contract/balance
GET /api/v4/event_contract/fund_flows
GET /api/v4/event_contract/fee/current
```

Responses are scoped to the authenticated account supplied by the gateway. Do not construct or expose internal account headers.
