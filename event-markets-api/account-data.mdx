---
title: "Event Markets Account Data"
sidebarTitle: "Account Data"
description: "Read authenticated Event Markets orders, trades, positions, assets, and account books."
---

These endpoints are private and require gateway authentication.

```http
GET /api/v4/event_markets/usertrades
GET /api/v4/event_markets/positions
GET /api/v4/event_markets/positions/{position_id}
POST /api/v4/event_markets/positions/{position_id}/close
GET /api/v4/event_markets/assets
GET /api/v4/event_markets/account_book
GET /api/v4/event_markets/claims
```

Responses are scoped to the authenticated account supplied by the gateway. Do not construct or expose internal account headers.

Use the user-trades endpoint to reconcile fills and refresh positions after an order changes state.
